Run club — everything u need to know

Every question we get asked before someone's first run, answered in one place.

How do I join?+
Register once, let us know if you're coming, and just show up.
When do we run & how often?+
Every Sunday, 6:00 AM sharp. Set that alarm.
Where do we meet?+
We announce the location in the group 2 days before every run!
How many kilometres will we run?+
Minimum 5 km — but beginners are absolutely welcome. Run, walk, jog — it doesn't matter. What matters is that you start.
Will I be left behind if I'm slow?+
Never. We run two paces — one for experienced runners, one for beginners — and there are people dedicated to managing each group. No one gets left behind.
Is there a pacer or group leader?+
Yes! Every run has pacers and group managers to guide you throughout. You're never on your own.
What if I need to stop midway?+
Totally okay. Our team is always around to help — don't stress about it, just show up and we'll take care of the rest.
Is there a separate group for beginners?+
Absolutely! We split into two pace groups so beginners always run at a comfortable pace with people supporting them the whole way.
What about water?+
We'd advise carrying your own bottle, but we've got you covered if you forget.
Should I eat before the run?+
Keep it light — a small snack 30–45 mins before is ideal. Avoid heavy meals right before you run.
Is there an age limit?+
None at all! Everyone is welcome — all ages, all fitness levels. This community is for everyone.
What happens after the run?+
The fun doesn't stop there! Post-run we have exciting activities, great vibes, new friends, and real connections. Trust us — it's the best part. See you on the road!
